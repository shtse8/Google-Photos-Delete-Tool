The extension

The extension runs only on photos.google.com, inside your browser. Your photos, thumbnails and fingerprints never leave your device. It has no server, no analytics and no telemetry. A Pro token is checked on your device and never sent to us; if you use Chrome sync, your browser keeps it on your other signed-in devices.

This website

If you allow it, this site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and Google Ads to measure which ads bring visitors. These set cookies and send Google your browser details, an online identifier and the pages you view. You choose in the cookie banner:

  • Accept all: analytics and ad measurement. If you accept all, the ad click id, which we also pass to Stripe as a reference (the checkout client_reference_id) so a purchase can be matched to an ad.
  • Analytics only: analytics cookies; no ad cookies, no ad personalisation.
  • Reject: no analytics or ad cookies. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland this is the default until you choose.

We never send Google your name, email or anything you type. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings. Your choice is stored in your browser (local storage key gpdt_consent). Google Analytics keeps event data for 14 months.

Stripe processes Pro payments. We never see your card number. Stripe sends us your email, country, the amount and the date. We use your email only to send your Pro token and to answer you about your order. We keep order records for 6 years because UK tax law requires it.
