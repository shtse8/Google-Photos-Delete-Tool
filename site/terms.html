Using the tool safely

The tool moves the photos you choose to Google Photos Trash, where Google keeps them for 60 days. "Empty trash afterwards" is optional and permanent. Run a dry run first and keep backups of photos you value. Google can change Google Photos at any time. When a change breaks the tool, it stops rather than guessing, and we work to fix it quickly.
